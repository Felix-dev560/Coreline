Ringline

Pick a name. Pick a person. Talk.

Ringline makes voice calls between people who have an account. Open it in any modern browser on your phone or computer. Nothing to install.

  1. Create an account with a username.
  2. Choose someone from the list and press Call.
  3. When they answer, you are talking. Your phone rings the same way when someone calls you, as long as Ringline is open.
